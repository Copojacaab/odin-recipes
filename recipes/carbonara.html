Carbonara Recipe

Description:

Carbonara is a traditional pasta dish from Rome, known for its creamy texture and rich, savory flavor.
It represent one of the most iconic tradition of Italian cuisine.

Ingredients:

  • Pasta (usually spaghetti)
  • Guanciale (cured pork cheek)
  • Eggs (mostly yolks)
  • Pecorino Romano chees
  • Freshly grond black pepper

Steps:

  1. Boil the pasta-bring a large pot of saltaed water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente.
  2. Cook the guanciale-in a pan, cook the guanciale over medium heat until it becomes crispy and golden,
    releasing its fat.
  3. Prepare the sauce-in a bowl, whisk the egg yolks with grated Pecorino Romano and freshlyl ground back pepper
    until creamy.
  4. Combine pasta and guanciale-drain the pasta (reserve a little cooking water),
    then toss it in the pan with the guanciale and its fat.
  5. Create the creamy sauce-remove the pan from direct heat, then add the egg-and-chees mixture.
    Stir quickly, adding a little pasta water if needed, untile the sauce becomes creamy
  6. Serve- the plate immediately sprinkle with extra Pecorino Romano and black pepper and enjoy!
