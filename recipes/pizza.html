Pizza Margherita

Description:

Pizza is a flat, circular dish baked at high heat,, with a soft center and crisp edges often enjoyed as a versatile and shaereable meal.

Ingredients:

  • Pizza dough (flour water, yeast, salt, olive oil)
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive oil
  • Salt

Steps:

  1. Prepare the dough - mix floour, yeast, water, salt and oil. Knead until smooth,
    then let it rise untile doubled in size.
  2. Preheat the oven - heat it to a very high temperature (about 250 C)
  3. Shape the base - roll or strech the dough into a flat, round shape
  4. Add the toppings - spread sauce, then add cheese and desired topping
  5. Bake - place in the hot oven and bake until the crust is golden and the cheese
    melted and bubby.
  6. Serve - let it cool briefly, slice and enjoy.
